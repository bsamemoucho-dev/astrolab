# Academic Research Library

This register tracks secondary academic sources useful for AstroLab's Hellenistic corpus work.

Academic studies are not primary sources. They may help locate passages, understand transmission, identify variants, document scholarly debate, propose interpretations, or frame reconstruction problems. They do not automatically prove what an ancient author said.

## Source Layer Types

- `primary_source`
- `critical_edition`
- `translation`
- `secondary_study`
- `thesis_dissertation`
- `commentary`

## Important Debates To Track

- Ptolemy's role in rationalizing or defending astrology.
- Translation and rewriting as active forces in Tetrabiblos transmission.
- Later reception of Ptolemy through Firmicus, Byzantine commentary, Arabic and medieval traditions.
- Distinction between textual transmission, interpretation, reconstruction, and later defense of astrology.
