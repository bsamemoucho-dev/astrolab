UNCERTAIN NATAL AXES AND CLIENT PRESENTATION

An approximate birth time can put an axis across a sign boundary. A nominal
longitude is still calculated at the supplied reference time, but it cannot
license an unqualified sign claim or an interpretation of that sign.

Causes
- Only ASC/MC carried explicit angle windows. Older saved calculations need the
  opposite DSC/IC window reconstructed by the exact 180-degree relationship.
- Some historical interpretation rules and factual comparisons did not enforce
  the evidence's declared instability. A nominal match was sufficient.
- A bounded sign detector could miss a factual angle/sign statement interrupted
  by an explanatory phrase. Coverage must include the actual sentence form.

Policy
- Keep numerical positions and uncertain source evidence for annotated reference.
- Apply the same determination policy to interpretation rules, plan inputs,
  factual claims, prose facts and personally timed angle contacts.
- Reject an unqualified nominal fact from unstable evidence. Text still produces
  undeclared_text_fact, with source_fact_undecidable as the diagnostic reason.
- Do not infer certainty from several agreeing symbols. Unknown or interval
  birth times do not supply a single angle or house.
- Invalid server prefixes remain blocking before any model correction attempt.

Editorial presentation
- Announce a natal configuration in prose only when the block owns an applicable
  interpretation rule. Keep other calculated aspects in the reference evidence.
- Assign the distinct existing planetary function to same-sign chapters. A shared
  sign or a contrast between two signs is not an interaction licence.
- The production selection is capped at four current and six future contacts,
  with time coverage retained and a preference for available interpretive matter.
  A contact starting later on the reference civil day remains UPCOMING and is
  explicitly labelled as later today. No astronomical instant is reclassified.
- The natal Moon/Mars square has no active general-reading rule in the existing
  catalogue. A relational-scoped rule must not be reused as a general licence.
  Developing that interpretation needs a separate documented catalogue change.

Print and identity
- Group chart distributions with their note, keep paragraphs together when they
  fit, and add linked contents and native page numbering in compatible Chromium.
- Retain a pale economical print cover, not the ink-heavy screen gradient.
- Read the name only from the supplied firstName; never derive it from an email
  or filename. The existing optional public field is moved before birth details.

Validation and limits
- Regression fixtures contain synthetic evidence or fictional birth data only.
- Full public service checks use an injected offline writer, never a paid API.
- Existing functional assertions remain unchanged. The plan's literal version
  expectation advances from 0.6.0 to 0.7.0 for method traceability. A multi-block
  prompt test now uses exact time: its former approximate fixture included
  undecidable MC/IC just to reach two blocks. Its two-block assertion is kept.
- Print QA can reuse already-produced text to measure layout. Such an artifact
  is a layout preview, not a factually corrected client dossier.
- These checks do not certify semantic originality or subjective writing quality.
- No stored dossier is rewritten, regenerated or notified by this change.

Additional boundary: margins wider than 60 minutes do not certify angle or
house signs under the endpoint/reference analysis. Full-rotation aliasing must
not turn equal sampled endpoints into a claim of stability. Positions remain
available as nominal measurements, not as an exhaustive sign-window list.
